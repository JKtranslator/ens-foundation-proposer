What changes, and what does not

Changes

  • The Foundation Safe gains one proposer: the KPK proposer Safe.
  • KPK can place transactions in the Foundation Safe's queue. They appear as proposed by "ENS Proposer - KPK managed".

Does not change

  • The owners, the 3-of-5 threshold, the modules and the guard.
  • Every transaction still needs three Foundation signatures before it can execute.
  • Nothing is sent on-chain. There is no gas fee.

Why the proposer is a Safe

The proposer is a Safe with six signers, two of which must agree: the five KPK signers of the Endowment's Manager Safe and the KPK Security Council Safe. KPK can change who proposes by changing those signers, without asking the Foundation to sign again. The proposer Safe holds no funds, has no modules and owns no other account. Proposing is its only role.

Why this page, rather than Safe{Wallet}

Safe{Wallet} does not accept a smart contract account as a proposer, and shows "Cannot add a smart contract account as proposer". Safe's Transaction Service, which stores proposers and serves them to Safe{Wallet}, does accept one when a current owner of the Safe signs for it. This page sends that signature to the Transaction Service directly. KPK registered and used a Safe proposer this way on Ethereum on 10 November 2026: Safe{Wallet} listed it as a proposer and showed its proposal in the queue.

    Your wallet asks for a signature, not a transaction. Before you sign, check that it shows a message of type Delegate with these values:

    In your wallet
    delegateAddress
    0xB1C6402137A3531c9E2d63985E0B76968C8243E8
    totp
    a number for the current hour; the signature is accepted for about an hour
    chainId
    1 (Ethereum)

    Afterwards

    Transactions proposed by KPK appear in the Foundation Safe's queue, marked as proposed by the KPK proposer Safe. Each one needs three of five Foundation signatures to execute, as any transaction does today.

    Any owner of the Foundation Safe can remove the proposer at any time in Safe{Wallet}, under Settings, Proposers. If the owner who signs here later leaves the Foundation Safe, the proposer stops working, and an owner who remains needs to sign this page again.

    This page holds no keys and stores nothing. It reads the two Safes from Safe's Transaction Service (api.safe.global), reads the proposer Safe's balance from a public Ethereum node (ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com), and sends your signature to the Transaction Service. It makes no other requests.
